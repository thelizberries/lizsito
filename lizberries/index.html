Bio

The Lizberries were born in the vibrant heart of Milan in 2010, embarking on a journey to become one of the most authentic and acclaimed tributes to the legendary Irish rock band The Cranberries. Their path closely mirrors that of the original band, extending beyond the Italian music scene to prestigious stages across Ireland, France, Switzerland, Romania, Germany and the United Kingdom.
Driven by a voice remarkably faithful to the unmistakable timbre of Dolores O'Riordan and a deep commitment to reproducing the unmistakable Cranberries sound, The Lizberries have earned a distinguished place among international tribute acts, widely regarded as one of the finest Cranberries tribute bands worldwide. From Milan's iconic Alcatraz to the historic Dolan's of Limerick, venues that once hosted The Cranberries themselves, The Lizberries recreate an atmosphere where fans can truly relive the magic of the original band...
