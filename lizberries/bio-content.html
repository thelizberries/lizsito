From the renowned Alcatraz in Milan to the prestigious Dolan's in Limerick, The Lizberries have graced stages that once hosted The Cranberries, creating an atmosphere where fans can relive the magic of the original band. Their extensive repertoire and faithful rendition of Cranberries classics ensure that audiences are treated to a mesmerizing two hour performance that captures the spirit of the legendary Irish rockers. The band's connection to the Cranberries' hometown is evident in their four tours of Ireland, where they took center stage in the most significant venues, notably Dolan's of Limerick. The Lizberries' commitment to their craft extends beyond the stage, as they actively participate in national gatherings of the official Cranberries fan club and collaborate on initiatives with Cranberries Italia. Looking to the future, The Lizberries are on the brink of creating a unique theatre show that delves into the history of the Cranberries. This ambitious project, in collaboration with an esteemed Irish Dance Academy and a renowned Italian DJ, promises to be a visual and auditory feast, offering fans a deeper connection to the Cranberries' legacy.
