Their extensive repertoire and meticulous attention to detail deliver a powerful two-hour live experience, capturing both the musical essence and emotional intensity of the legendary Irish rockers. Their strong bond with the Cranberries' homeland is reflected in four tours of Ireland, performing on some of the country's most important stages, with Dolan's of Limerick standing out as a symbolic cornerstone of their journey. Beyond live performances, The Lizberries actively engage with the Cranberries' legacy by participating in national gatherings of the official Cranberries Fan Club and collaborating with Cranberries Italia on special initiatives.
In 2024, The Lizberries expanded their artistic vision by creating, in collaboration with Gens D'Ys (Irish dance academy), Andrea Rock, and Deborah Pini, the theatrical show “The Queen of Limerick”, dedicated to the story of The Cranberries. The production will be performed in Ireland in May 2026, returning the tribute to the band's native land. The year 2026 promises an intense international touring schedule, with confirmed dates in the UK, Ireland, Switzerland, Slovenia, Holland and Spain, and additional shows currently in development. These achievements further affirm the exceptional quality of The Lizberries' show-faithful to the spirit of The Cranberries, respectful of their musical legacy, and a heartfelt homage to the unforgettable, ethereal voice of Dolores O'Riordan.
