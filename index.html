Bio

We are the Lizards and we are a rock band from Milan born in 2004. Initially, still inexperienced, we work full time on our unreleased songs. In 2011, to increase our visibility, we combine the work on our songs with our passion for the music of the Cranberries and we start a parallel project, The Lizberries tribute to the Irish band. In a short time, thanks to the singer's voice similar to that of Dolores, to a sound taken care of in the smallest details and to our harmony, we find ourselves playing on prestigious stages, even at an international level, becoming the best tribute at a European level...
